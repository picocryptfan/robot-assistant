1.2.2: "open car mode", « ouvre le mode voiture » and "carmode" now start car mode, and "close car mode" or « ferme le mode voiture » ends it. Before, Robot sent them to the model, which said it has no car mode. A fact you asked Robot to remember is now found when you ask about it, even while memory holds only a few items.
