Robot™ is a complete voice assistant that doesn't come from Google or Apple. Say "hey robot", ask for what you need, and it answers out loud. What you say is heard, understood and answered on the phone. Nothing you say is sent anywhere unless you ask for it.

I built it because there wasn't one. My phone runs GrapheneOS with no Google account, and every assistant that could actually do things came from Google or Apple.

WHAT IT DOES
• Timers and alarms, also from the lock screen, and your calendar
• Today: a home page for your day (off until you turn it on), a Calendar tab, a widget, news feeds you add, private events
• Drafts texts and emails in the apps you choose; you press Send
• Opens your maps app with a search or route ready
• Keeps notes and finds them
• Answers questions about what's on your screen, or a photo, once the phone is unlocked
• Answers everyday questions with Gemma, Google's open model, on the phone
• The weather from Open-Meteo, only when you ask or tap it
• Checks battery, storage and memory; helps free space
• Conversation mode: transcripts with speaker names, live French and English translation, summaries
• Your own wake words, voice speed, and a People list for the names you use
• Skills: teach routines by voice, plan many things at once, share them signed
• Lists by voice, files in a question, car screen, Robot to Robot
• New in 1.4: a ready reply to "what time do you land?"; web pages summarized; articles, notes and PDFs read aloud; recipe steps one at a time; Wikipedia on the phone; undo; scam warnings
• Phone lock: covers other apps for times you set, by voice or in Settings. Off until you set it up
• Robot Files: receipts, bills and documents filed on the phone, encrypted. Ask what you spent or what's due
• An encrypted backup in one file you keep, files included; never uploaded
• Checks GitHub for updates daily; you install them, unless you turn on automatic installs

LANGUAGES
Understands and speaks English and Québec French. Reads other languages and answers in English or French.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Wake word, speech recognition, the AI model and the voice all run on the phone
• Works offline once its model files are downloaded
• Goes online only for what you ask (the weather, a search, a download), the daily update check and its download on Wi-Fi, the weekly Termux check once Termux is set up, news feeds you add, and routines you allow to. Each can be turned off in Settings
• Each action can be set to Just do it, Ask first, or Off
• No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no account, no Google Play services
• Audio is never saved; message and screen content is never logged
• Phone lock's accessibility service sees only which app is in front

OPTIONAL: HELPERS
For a question that needs a bigger model, Robot can pass that one question to an AI you choose: a paid service with your API key, another AI's app on your phone (you press Send there), or your own server.

REQUIREMENTS
• Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL and 10 Pro Fold run the model on the TPU (tested on the Pixel 10 Pro)
• Pixel 6 to 9, their a models including the 10a, Fold and Tablet work more slowly on the GPU or CPU
• Pixel 11 in beta. Galaxy and other Android 14 phones should run; untested so far
• Pixels come first because GrapheneOS runs on them: keeping Google off the phone is the point
• Android 14 or newer; built and tested on GrapheneOS
• 3.6 to 4.3 GB free for model and voice files, depending on the phone, downloaded once and checked by SHA-256
• The model uses about 2 GB of memory while loaded

SETUP
Robot becomes your default digital assistant (Settings › Apps › Default apps). Its setup screen checks everything.
