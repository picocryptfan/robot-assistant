How to descale a Breville Barista Express

By Sam Writer · 2 March 2026

Scale builds up inside every espresso machine. The Barista Express has a dedicated descale mode, and running it every two to three months keeps the water flowing and the coffee tasting right.

SECRET-MATH

What you need

  • Breville descaling powder, or citric acid
  • A container that holds at least 2 litres

Steps

  1. Empty the drip tray and fill the tank with the solution.
  2. Press and hold the 1 CUP and 2 CUP buttons together for 5 seconds.
  3. Turn the dial to hot water, then to steam, and let the tank run empty.
